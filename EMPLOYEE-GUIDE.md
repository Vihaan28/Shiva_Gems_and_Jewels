# Employee Guide — Managing the Shiva Gems and Jewels Website

This guide is for anyone adding, editing or removing jewellery on the
website. You do **not** need to know any coding. Everything below is done
in your web browser.

---

## 1. How to log in

1. Go to **www.mywebsite.com/admin** (replace with the real website
   address once it's live).
2. Enter the email and password you were given, or click **"Forgot
   password?"** if this is your first time and you have an invite email.
3. You will see the **dashboard** with sections down the left side:
   **Products**, **Categories**, and **Site Settings**.

---

## 2. How to add a product

1. Click **Products** in the left menu.
2. Click **New Product** (top right).
3. Fill in:
   - **Product Name** — e.g. "Diamond Solitaire Ring"
   - **Unique ID** — a short code with no spaces, e.g. `diamond-ring-05`.
     Once you publish, avoid changing this.
   - **Category** — pick from the dropdown.
   - **Description** — a short sentence about the piece.
   - **Price Type** — choose *Price on Request*, *Fixed Price*, or
     *Starting From*.
   - **Price** — only needed for Fixed Price / Starting From. Enter
     numbers only, e.g. `450000`. The website adds ₹ and commas for you.
   - **Primary Image** — click the box, then **Upload** and choose a
     photo from your computer.
   - **Additional Images** (optional) — click **Add** to include more
     photos of the same piece.
   - **Featured / Show on Homepage / Bridal Product / Available** —
     toggle these on or off as needed.
   - **Display Order** — lower numbers show first (e.g. 1 shows before 2).
4. Click **Publish** at the top right.
5. Give it 1–2 minutes — the website rebuilds automatically.

## 3. How to upload product images

- While editing a product, click the **Primary Image** box.
- Click **Upload**, then choose a photo from your computer (or drag and
  drop it into the box).
- To add more photos of the same piece, use **Additional Images** →
  **Add**.

## 4. How to set a primary image

The **Primary Image** field is always the main photo shown on the
product card and at the top of the product page. To change it, click the
image box and upload or choose a different photo.

## 5. How to add additional images

Open the product, scroll to **Additional Images**, click **Add**, and
upload each extra photo. These appear as the thumbnail strip on the
product's detail page.

## 6. How to change a price

1. Open the product from the **Products** list.
2. Update the **Price** field (numbers only).
3. Click **Publish**.

## 7. How to change price type

1. Open the product.
2. Change the **Price Type** dropdown to *Price on Request*, *Fixed
   Price*, or *Starting From*.
3. Click **Publish**.

## 8. How to edit a product

1. Click **Products**.
2. Click the product you want to change.
3. Edit any field.
4. Click **Publish**.

## 9. How to hide a product (without deleting it)

1. Open the product.
2. Turn **Published** off.
3. Click **Publish** (this saves the change, even though the product
   itself becomes hidden from the live site).
4. To bring it back later, turn **Published** back on.

## 10. How to delete a product

1. Open the product.
2. Click the small menu (⋮ or **Delete entry**) near the top of the
   editor.
3. Confirm deletion.

**Note:** deleting is permanent. If you just want it off the site
temporarily, use **Hide** (step 9) instead.

## 11. How to create a category

1. Click **Categories** in the left menu.
2. Click **New Category**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Category Name** — e.g. "Rings"
   - **Slug** — lowercase, no spaces, e.g. `rings`
   - **Description** — a short sentence about the collection.
   - **Hero Image** — the large banner photo for that collection page.
   - **Display Order** — where it appears among other categories.
4. Click **Publish**. The website automatically creates a working page
   for this category — nobody needs to build a new page by hand.

## 12. How to edit a category

1. Click **Categories**.
2. Click the category you want to change.
3. Edit the fields.
4. Click **Publish**.

## 13. How to change homepage content

1. Click **Site Settings** → **Homepage Content**.
2. Edit the hero heading, description, image, or "Our Story" text.
   (For headings that span two lines, put a `|` character where you
   want the line to break.)
3. Click **Save**.

## 14. How to change phone numbers

1. Click **Site Settings** → **Contact Details**.
2. Update **Phone Number 1 / 2 / 3**.
3. Click **Save**. All pages update automatically.

## 15. How to change the email address

1. Click **Site Settings** → **Contact Details**.
2. Update **Email Address**.
3. Click **Save**.

## 16. How to publish changes

Every screen has a **Publish** (for Products/Categories) or **Save**
(for Site Settings) button in the top right. Click it whenever you're
done editing — nothing goes live until you do.

## 17. How to check if changes are live

1. Wait 1–2 minutes after publishing.
2. Open the live website in a new browser tab.
3. Refresh the page (or press Ctrl+Shift+R / Cmd+Shift+R for a full
   refresh) and check your change appears.

If it doesn't appear after a few minutes, tell your admin/developer —
see **ADMIN-GUIDE.md** for troubleshooting.
